Beginners course
3-week curriculum overview (linked to the full page) ▾

The full Beginners curriculum is kept up to date on: Beginners Photography Classes.

If you’re deciding whether it’s right for you, the key promise is simple: understand the settings, see the result, then repeat a clear process until it becomes natural.

  • Week 1: camera confidence (moving beyond Auto)
  • Week 2: exposure and focus (what to change and why)
  • Week 3: composition, light and repeatable practice

Note: the detailed breakdown lives on the course page so it remains consistent across all date listings.

Lightroom editing
Lightroom Photo Editing Course (3 weeks) — what you’ll be able to do ▾

Course page (full details and dates): Lightroom Photo Editing Course — Coventry.

  • Build a simple, repeatable editing workflow (import → develop → export)
  • Improve colour, contrast and sharpness without “over-editing”
  • Use local adjustments to guide attention and refine the subject
  • Export correctly for printing, sharing and web use

Requirements: laptop capable of running Lightroom + Adobe Photography plan.

External reference (opens new window): Adobe Photography plan.

RPS mentoring
RPS distinctions — what the mentoring supports (1–12 months) ▾

Full details: RPS Courses, Mentoring & Distinctions.

  • Panel planning: intent, consistency, sequencing and story
  • Technical improvement: exposure, focus, colour management and print readiness
  • Project feedback: what to change, how to reshoot, how to refine
  • Flexible duration: from a fast one-month push to a structured 6–12 month build

External references (open new window): Royal Photographic Society (RPS)  •  RPS Distinctions overview.

Local practice
Coventry practice ideas (so you improve between sessions) ▾
  • War Memorial Park: exposure practice + focus modes with moving subjects.
  • Cathedral Quarter: composition, lines, symmetry and low-light technique.
  • Canal Basin: reflections, leading lines and golden hour light.
  • Allesley Park: depth of field, foreground interest and subject isolation.
